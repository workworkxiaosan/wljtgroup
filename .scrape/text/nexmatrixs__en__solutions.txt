FAQ
Before You Engage
What is a "real overseas device", and how is it different from a VPN?
We use real overseas devices with local carrier SIM cards, so IP, device fingerprint, and registration environment all match local users. A VPN merely disguises the IP on a domestic device — the fingerprint, timezone, and system language still reveal a domestic environment, which platform algorithms can detect and down-rank, directly affecting reach.
How long until results? Can you guarantee order volume?
Short-video accounts need 2–3 months to build weight; typically noticeable exposure and follower growth appear in 3–6 months, with steady inquiries after 6 months. We do not promise a specific order count — we provide high-quality exposure and precise traffic, while final conversion depends on your product competitiveness, pricing, and sales follow-up.
Can I run just one platform?
Absolutely. All three platforms can be contracted independently, with no bundling. On a limited budget we suggest starting with TikTok (fastest ramp, strongest reach support), then expanding to Instagram or YouTube once validated. Starting with 1–2 platforms is the sensible choice.
We're B2B — is short-video marketing suitable?
Very suitable. Overseas B2B buyers (importers, traders, retail procurement) increasingly find suppliers via TikTok and Instagram. Short video vividly demonstrates factory scale, capacity, and QC standards to build trust — B2B inquiry conversion is already strong in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.
Do page prices match the final contract?
Page prices are indicative starting points; actual fees may vary with industry specifics, shooting complexity, and language needs. We offer a free initial consultation and issue a formal quote valid for 30 days — all prices tax-inclusive with no hidden fees.
What if an account has issues during the contract?
Account anomalies (bans, algorithm down-ranking) are occasional risks in short-video ops. Issues caused by our operations are handled and restored for free; issues from client-requested non-compliant content or platform policy changes are resolved by mutual agreement. Devices are managed by us during the contract and handed over per the agreement at term end.
What is the scope of your service?
We handle: shooting, editing, publishing, account maintenance, basic routing (to your D2C site / private domain), and monthly reporting. We do not handle: inquiry conversations, price negotiation, or after-sales (your sales team follows up). We do not promise order counts but provide high-quality exposure and precise traffic. We recommend a 6-month minimum term, ~half a day of on-site shooting per month, with footage copyright owned by the client.
